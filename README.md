## 📌 Key Focus Areas & Field Observations

### 📡 Telecom & Optical Fiber Communication
- **OFC Backbone:** Handled Optical Fiber Cable testing, fusion splicing, and OTDR fault-location techniques[cite: 1].
- **Railway Exchanges & Networks:** Operational understanding of Electronic Exchanges, Passenger Information Systems (PIS), and RailWire/Rail-Net networks[cite: 1].

### 🛡️ Kavach (Indigenous Automatic Train Protection - ATP)
- **SIL-4 Compliance:** In-depth study of RDSO's SIL-4 standard Kavach architecture (Loco KAVACH & Station KAVACH)[cite: 1].
- **RF & Location Tracking:** Analyzed 400 MHz UHF radio communications, RFID tag balises for absolute positioning, and dynamic braking profiles[cite: 1].

### 🚥 Signaling & Interlocking
- **Electronic & Relay Interlocking:** Studied fail-safe hardwired logic in Relay Rooms and Microprocessor-based Electronic Interlocking (EI) VDUs[cite: 1].
- **Train Detection:** Maintenance and operation of DC Track Circuits, AFTC, and Axle Counters[cite: 1].
